## Overview

**WorldEndArchive V2** is a digital Noah's Ark for human knowledge - small enough to fit in your pocket.

The first archive gathered the web as it was. This one distills it. Every page is rewritten in plain words, so
that anyone can understand it when there is no expert left to ask. In English and in German.

How to make fire. How to make water safe to drink. How to stop bleeding. Why penicillin changed everything,
how bread is baked, how the stars can guide you home. What took humanity millennia to learn, kept where it
cannot be lost.

No server. No signal. No power grid. Just a phone and the knowledge of those who came before.

## What it holds

*   **Survival** - fire, shelter, water, food, finding your way, being found.
*   **First aid** - what to do in the minutes that matter.
*   **Discoveries** - science, medicine and the ideas that built our world.
*   **Craft** - farming, preserving food, tools, knots, recipes.
*   **History & nature** - where we come from, and what grows around us.

## Open the Archive

<details>
<summary><strong>In your browser</strong></summary>

Visit the archive's GitHub Pages site. Open it once while the world is still connected - from then on it
stays with you, even offline. "Add to Home Screen" keeps it one tap away.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>On Android</strong></summary>

Download `WorldEndArchive.apk` from the [latest release](../../releases/latest) and install it.
Everything is inside. It will never need the internet again.

It also carries guides for the first hours of darkness: keeping your phone alive, what matters first,
safe water, what to do when lost, and how to call for help.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Keep it current</strong></summary>

The whole archive lives in a single file: `reader/data/archive.db`.
Replace it with a newer one and push - the website and the app rebuild themselves.

</details>
