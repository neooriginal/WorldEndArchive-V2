## Where the knowledge comes from

Every entry is a plain-language rewrite of one source. Each row keeps its origin in the `source_title`,
`source_url` and `license` columns, so attribution travels with the data.

| Source | License | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| [Wikipedia](https://www.wikipedia.org) (English snapshot) | CC BY-SA 4.0 | discoveries, science, history, nature, medicine, places, people |
| [Wikibooks](https://www.wikibooks.org) (English and German) | CC BY-SA 4.0 | how-tos, textbooks, the Cookbook |
| US Army field manuals FM 3-05.70, FM 21-76-1, FM 4-25.11 (via archive.org) | Public domain (US government works) | survival, first aid |

Entries were written and translated (English ↔ German) by AI models and then checked by other models against the
source. Wording therefore differs from the originals; the facts come from them.

## No warranty - especially for medical and survival content

The archive is a best-effort summary, not professional advice. Survival and first-aid entries are partly based on
manuals from 1999-2002; guidance changes over time. When real help is reachable, use it.
